# Freeman Ideas

This folder is the conceptual workshop for Freeman.

Use it for non-production thinking that may influence the website, brand, project storytelling, business development, or Freeman's broader point of view.

## What belongs here

- design and brand concepts
- project-storytelling frameworks
- precedent studies
- atmosphere, material, and architectural references
- experimental page concepts
- language worth preserving
- ideas that may later become site features or field practices

## Working rule

Capture freely here. Promote selectively.

An idea should move into production only when it is specific enough to affect the real Freeman system.

## Current governing threads

- Built for Generations
- Finished Result ↔ Field Evidence
- The Freeman Record
- Carry the Idea Through
- Freeman as steward of intent between design and construction
