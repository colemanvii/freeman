## 2. Principles

**Carry over (all INFERRED)**

1. Commit to a named series and return to it.
2. Document work already happening.
3. Show process as the evidence for quality.
4. Film an ambition before it is realised, so the milestone has a history.
5. Leave the friction in.
6. Keep a lighter fallback format so cadence never depends on a full production.
7. Own the archive on your own site.
8. Aim for depth with a core audience over reach.

**Do not carry over**

- Repeat, impulse-purchase consumer model; drop mechanics.
- Aspirational founder lifestyle as content.
- Freedom to film: a contractor works on client-owned sites, under approvals and safety rules.
- A large pre-existing audience at launch.
- The aesthetic.
