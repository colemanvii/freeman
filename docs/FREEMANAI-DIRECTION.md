## The idea

**A company you can read.**

FreemanAI should make the collective intelligence of a construction company immediate, visible, and useful. Not a dashboard. Not a chat window dressed as one. The company becomes a **living drawing set**: ask a question, and the relevant part of Freeman takes shape.

The metaphor serves the work. It is familiar to builders, precise enough for serious decisions, and memorable without theatrical excess.

## Design language

- **Sheets, not screens.** A question produces the appropriate drawing: company pulse, project history, precedent, or decision. A title block establishes context, role, sources, date, and status.
- **Evidence is visible.** Numbered detail bubbles open the underlying record. A red revision cloud marks a contradiction; an unresolved fact remains unresolved.
- **Time is a material.** Drag the date and watch a project assemble room by room. Field notes arrive when they happened. Never imply knowledge the selected moment did not contain.
- **The field is first-class.** A superintendent can capture an observation quickly on a phone; it moves through people and decisions without losing its origin.
- **Decisions leave an impression.** The Freeman eagle stamps a confirmed decision. Its meaning comes from the workflow, not decoration.

## Feeling

Precise, tactile, intelligent, quietly playful. Warm paper, dark ink, and disciplined Freeman red. Typography and drawing conventions do the heavy lifting; motion reveals causality. Sound, if used, should feel made of pencil, paper, tools, and human activity—not generic digital effects.

**Memorable because it explains something difficult beautifully.**

## Non-negotiables

Answers carry their evidence, timestamp, and uncertainty. Privacy is designed in, not painted on. No fabricated live company activity, decorative data, generic SaaS cards, glowing AI graphics, or animations that delay work. Mobile use must hold up under field conditions.

## What is approved—and what isn't

**Approved:** the creative model, drawing-set metaphor, information behavior, and overall visual/interaction direction represented by Exploration 002.

**Not approved as production software:** the scripted sample answers, simulated role switcher, local-only records, voice fallback, and fictional metrics. They demonstrate a vision. They are not connected intelligence or secure workflows.
