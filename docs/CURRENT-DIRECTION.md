# Current Direction

*Freeman creative direction — 22 October 2026. A working reference, not a declaration that every item is deployed.*

## Position

**Built for Generations.**

Freeman builds ambitious hospitality places with construction judgment, relationships, and care for what endures. Speak to discerning restaurateurs, developers, architects, and operators. Be specific; avoid self-congratulation and generic claims of excellence.

**Approved company opening**

> We build hospitality spaces for people with a clear vision of what they want to create.

> From early coordination through opening day, we bring the relationships, judgment, and construction expertise necessary to turn ambitious concepts into enduring places.

## Editorial system

- Pair **finished places** with **field evidence**: photographs, decisions, drawings, coordination, and people doing the work.
- The website should feel authored, quiet, and precise — more like a printed institutional composition than a contractor template.
- Desktop V5 favors deliberate, no-scroll compositions. Mobile adapts for legibility and usability; never force content into an unreadable frame.
- Restrained typography, warm light/dark palettes, hairlines, crosshair details, and Freeman red only when it means something.
- Motion must clarify a gesture or an identity, never become a disconnected flourish. The eagle is a stamp, not a decorative loading trick.
- No fake coordinates, fabricated dates, invented project facts, stock construction language, or unverified credits.
- No card-heavy SaaS aesthetic, generic serif makeover, gratuitous gradients, shadows, or floating UI.

## Live website versus product exploration

The public site prioritizes editorial restraint. **FreemanAI is a different kind of surface:** interactive, useful in the field, and permitted to respond dynamically to questions and tasks while retaining the same Freeman character.

Ideas retired or held for study are **not** standing requirements: the older “result proves taste” line, broad Fieldwork/Notes experiments, and unapproved page concepts remain in the archive. Do not restore them simply because they appear in an older branch or manifesto.

The [October 4 Design Doctrine](../DESIGN-DOCTRINE.md) documents an important earlier foundation. It remains worth reading but is not a current implementation checklist.

## Review test

Does this reveal how Freeman thinks or merely imitate a beautiful website? Does it help the right person understand the work? Is every stated fact defensible? If not, revise.
