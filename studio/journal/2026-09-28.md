# 2026-09-28

## What changed

The Freeman role widened today.

The opportunity is not only to document completed construction or improve the website. There may be a useful collaboration between Cole's eye and Gabe's builder judgment earlier in the process.

### Working model

**Cole**
reference / atmosphere / drawing / visual narrative / documentation / cultural positioning

**Gabe**
constructability / cost / sequencing / trades / land / developer relationships / execution

Not designer + builder as formal titles. More simply: eye + execution.

## Cabin idea

A small cabin on Gabe's property could become a real test:

Reference → Sketch → Gabe redline → Detail → Construction → Finished result

Fay Jones is the first reference point, especially the roof, threshold, custom woodworking and integrated cabinetry.

The goal is not to reproduce a Fay Jones house. It is to understand what creates the feeling and see what a Freeman version becomes under real constraints.

## Larger business possibility

Gabe raised the idea of getting involved with people developing golf courses and related destination projects.

That suggests a longer-term lane for Freeman around:
- golf communities
- clubs
- hospitality-residential development
- cabins / guest houses
- place-making
- early builder participation

The interesting position is not "contractor waiting for plans." It is a builder with a clear point of view about place, hospitality, materials, craft and residential experience.
