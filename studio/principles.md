# Working Principles

These are provisional. They should change as the work teaches us more.

## 01 — Show the work
Finished photography matters more when the decisions and construction behind it remain visible.

## 02 — Build for belonging
A house is not only an object. Thresholds, rooms, landscape, material and movement determine whether a place feels hospitable.

## 03 — Let the builder's judgment show
Freeman's point of view should be visible through decisions: what gets simplified, protected, rebuilt, detailed, or left alone.

## 04 — Study before styling
Use precedent to understand why something works, not to copy its appearance.

## 05 — Details carry conviction
Cabinetry, masonry, trim, hardware, proportion and transitions often communicate more than brand language.

## 06 — Document upstream
The record begins with reference, sketch and conversation — not only once construction starts.

## 07 — Avoid theater
Ordinary field evidence is more credible than staged "craft" content.

## 08 — Built for Generations
The phrase should be proven through material choices, stewardship, durability, relationships and finished work.
