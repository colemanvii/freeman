# Project Monograph 001: asset inventory

Audited 20 Oct 2026 across every branch of `colemanvii/freeman` (all image, PDF and video objects in history), the production release branch `release/approved-v5-2026-10-18` (44cb8cc, byte-identical to live freemangc.com for `app.js`, `styles.css` and `records.js`), and the Project Story 001 and Field Notes studies. No image carries EXIF capture data.

## Verdict

- **Finished photography:** good for four hospitality projects, excellent for two (Beetlecat, The Optimist). All of it was photographed by Jared Swafford, except O-Ku, Bocado and Dalton, which are credited to Freeman GC.
- **Construction evidence:** **none, for any project.** No dated field photograph, drawing, schedule, daily log or closeout record is on file anywhere in the repository.
- **Editorial assets:** one archive drawing (The Optimist sign) and the published project accounts in `records.js`. Everything else is either archive reference material with no project or date, or AI-generated.

## Why Beetlecat

The result can prove taste only where the photograph shows Freeman's own work. Beetlecat is the only strongly photographed hospitality project whose published account claims the whole build. The Optimist has more and finer photographs, but its published scope is one bar inside a room designed and built out by others, so a monograph built on those photographs would credit Freeman for work the record does not support. The Optimist is the right second monograph once its Oyster Bar scope is matched to specific photographs.

## Needed to complete the standard (any project)

1. Dated field photographs from fixed positions, especially before work is concealed (the capture protocol in `PROJECT-RECORD.md`).
2. Start and handover dates.
3. Drawings: shop, fabrication or construction drawings for the signature pieces.
4. A design credit where one exists, agreed with the designer.
5. A client statement in the client's own words.
6. Higher-resolution originals from the photographer, and permission to publish crops of their photographs.
