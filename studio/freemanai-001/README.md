## Concept

One page with three verbs, and the verbs are the navigation.

- **See.** What is true now for this role: three figures, each with its source and date; numbered signals in priority order; the jobs register.
- **Ask.** Answers composed only from the record.
  - Every sentence cites its sources, and opening a source shows the cited passage marked in orange.
  - Each answer is labelled with one of four states: from the record, the record disagrees, restricted, or not on file. It never guesses.
  - An answer can turn into an action.
- **Act.** Capture from the field (kind, place, note, photo, dictation), give it one owner and a date, and decide.
  - Every change adds to the record's trail.
  - The new item appears in the owner's See and Act straight away.

The four roles see one company through different scopes:

| Role | Scope | Has | Lands on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Owner | Everything | — | Decisions waiting on them |
| Controller | Finance | — | Pay apps, aging, waivers |
| Project manager | Their jobs | No finance | Critical path |
| Superintendent | One site | Phone first | Capture before the queue |

Identity: the ACCESS plate (Monaco without smoothing, chalk paper, hairlines). Orange marks only signals, the active verb and cited evidence.

## Sample data

Everything is fictional: the projects (2604–2615), the people, the companies and the figures. The only real figure is the $12M 2026 goal. Real staff names were left out on purpose, so that no invented facts are attached to real people.

## Not real yet
