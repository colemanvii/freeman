# The Record — Exploration 001

An archive that happens to be a map. This standalone study is for the interaction model and the art direction. It is not the complete archive.

- Branch `study/the-record-001`, cut from the production release (`132d925`). It has no upstream. Push it only with an explicit refspec.
- Page: `v5/studies/the-record-001/`. The local preview config is `the-record-001` on port 8825.
- Production, `main`, the More/ETCETERA page and every project page are untouched.

## What a visitor sees

1. **The overview.** A survey drawing of north Georgia in warm paper and ink. It shows the state line, county lines, interstates, rivers, reservoirs, the Atlanta and Decatur city limits, and town names. The title "Places we've helped build." sits on the sheet, and a register of all 15 projects fills the title block. Red concentric marks show where the work is.
2. **Exploring.** Drag, scroll, pinch, use the + / − / ALL controls, or use the arrow and +/− keys. Groups of marks show a count and a site name, such as "Barnsley Resort 3" or "South Downtown 3".
   - Selecting a group zooms in until its projects separate.
   - Several projects at one site (Broad St SW, Barnsley Resort) open a callout that lists them.
   - Closer in, the drawing adds neighbourhood names and streets.
   - The title steps back once you zoom in, and returns at the overview.
3. **A record.** Selecting a mark, a register line or a search result opens a record sheet beside the map. The map stays visible with the project centred.
   - The sheet shows the number, type, name, published address and coordinates (or the approximate area), the photographs, client and credits, and the published account.
   - A "not yet on file" line names what is missing.
   - "Nearest in the record" gives real distances, and "Full project" links to the project page.
   - Escape, ← MAP or the browser's back button returns you to the exact map position and zoom you left.
4. **Find.** Search by project, client, type, town or neighbourhood. Press / to focus the search field.
5. **The time rule.** A "Watch it grow" control and a draggable year rule (with keyboard support) reveal projects by verified completion year. Undated projects stay on the map, drawn quietly while you look into the past.
6. **Phones.** The map fills the screen. The register opens from the bottom, and records rise as a scrolling sheet over the lower two-thirds, so the project stays visible on the map above.

## Integrity of the record

- **Identity, photographs and stories** come from `/records.js`, the site's own register. Nothing new is written about any project. Beetlecat's account is the published one, marked as such.
- **Places** are in `sites.js`, each with its source. Venue addresses were cross-checked between OpenStreetMap/Nominatim and news or listing sources on 22 Oct 2026.
  - *At the site*: Beetlecat, The Optimist, O-Ku, Savi Provisions (Emory Village), Brewhouse Cafe / Mule Train / Broad Street BBQ (Broad St SW, South Downtown), and the three Barnsley Resort projects (resort coordinates; the pool and pub are not mapped separately).
  - *Approximate area* (dashed ring; no address or coordinates shown): La Cresta on Main and Dalton Surgery Center by policy (residential, healthcare); Muchacho Decatur and Azalea Marketplace because their sites are not confirmed.
  - *Not placed*: Bocado Pizza. It had several locations (West Midtown, closed 2021; Sandy Springs, 2024), and which one Freeman built is unknown. It stays in the register as "not placed".
- **Years.** No completion year is on file for any project. The photographs carry no capture dates, and the one legacy "Complete 2024" credit was already ruled unconfirmed in Project Story 001. Per Cole's direction, the rule is fully built and honestly empty: "No completion years are on file yet" and "Year not on file 15". To bring it to life, add verified years to `RECORD_SITES.years` in `sites.js` (for example `'beetlecat': 2019`). Nothing else changes.

## Base map

`geo.js` is generated by `build-geo.js` from:

- US Census state and county lines via `us-atlas@3.0.1` (`counties-10m.json`).
- © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via the Overpass API, fetched 22 Oct 2026:
  - `relation[route=road][network="US:I"]` (interstates)
  - `way[waterway=river][name]` (rivers)
  - `node[place~"^(city|town)$"]` plus selected Atlanta neighbourhoods (names)
  - named reservoirs, `natural=water` (Lanier, Allatoona, Carters, Blue Ridge, Nottely, Chatuge, Weiss)
  - Atlanta and Decatur `admin_level=8` (city limits)
  - `trunk|primary|secondary|tertiary` streets around Atlanta, Blue Ridge, Dalton and Adairsville (close-zoom detail)

The bounding box is 33.35–35.15° N, 83.45–85.75° W. The projection is a local equirectangular projection about 34.15° N, 84.6° W, which is accurate to well under 1% across the region.
