The way we see it, construction is a service industry no different than a high end restaurant or a boutique hotel. The process of building should be as enjoyable as the end product, resulting in relationships and projects that are built for generations.

At Freeman General Contracting, relationships are the bedrock of our work. We bring the same values of respect, integrity, and commitment from our personal lives to our interactions with clients, subcontractors, suppliers, architects, and engineers. These genuine connections form the foundation for every project.

Design + Build

What is the typical timeline for a commercial construction project?

Freeman General Contracting offers expert Construction Management services, providing comprehensive oversight and coordination throughout the entire construction process. Our approach ensures that projects are completed on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.

How do you ensure projects stay on budget?

We ensure projects stay on budget through meticulous planning, detailed cost estimation, and regular monitoring of expenses. Transparent communication with clients and proactive problem-solving also play key roles in budget management.

What measures do you take to ensure site safety?

Site safety is a top priority for us. We implement strict safety protocols, conduct regular training, and ensure all workers comply with safety regulations.

How do you manage subcontractors and suppliers?

We manage subcontractors and suppliers through rigorous vetting, clear contracts, and ongoing communication. We ensure they meet our quality standards and adhere to project schedules and specifications.
