Built for Generations — A series of conversations

Conversations on what lasts.

Draft introduction
For editorial review

Long conversations with people who have spent their lives making things — buildings, furniture, companies, institutions — and who have learned, often the hard way, what holds.

We are builders. Construction is our vantage point, not our limit. We are interested in the decisions behind durable work: the discipline of a craft, the weight of responsibility, the failures that were survived, and what a person owes to the people who will inherit what they leave behind.

Each conversation is recorded in full, photographed where the work happens, and published when it is ready — not on a schedule.

About the series

Proposed format

Each entry has four parts: a written introduction, documentary photographs, the full recorded conversation, and a short film where the work itself is worth seeing. Nothing is cut for length. Edits are made for clarity and are noted.

New conversations are added to the index above as they are finished. The index is meant to grow for years, so it stays a plain numbered list.
