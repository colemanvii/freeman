# Built for Generations — Design Study 002

Study 001 is kept unchanged one folder up (`../index.html`) for comparison.

Study 002 is built on the live Freeman V5 system (freemangc.com/styles.css, read 19 Oct 2026), not an approximation of it:

- Tokens: paper `#fff9f1`, ink `#332e20`, muted `#706659`, hair `#d5cec3`, orange `#f93822`, Monaco, font smoothing off, both crosshair cursors.
- Shell: header 28px from the top with 30px insets; content between 94px and 88px; legal text bottom-left and `BUILT FOR GENERATIONS™` bottom-right, as on the home page.
- Nav: the V5 section line with Conversations added as a fourth section in orange, and `× CLOSE` on the right.
- Interaction: instant word-level orange hover, no transitions. Index rows follow the projects index (number turns orange, name gets the highlight block).
- Detail page: the V5 project gallery pattern (220px info column + stage + `← 01 / 06 →`), with the conversation as six sheets.

What belongs to BFG only: the stacked title card, the portrait plate (a viewfinder centred on Freeman's crosshair), the Q&A ledger, the audio dimension line, and placing image and caption off-axis on each sheet.

All participants, titles and texts are editorial examples.
