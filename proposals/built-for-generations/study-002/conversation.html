02 / Introduction

Example text

A working title for a conversation about the hours a design spends on paper before anyone builds it, and what that patience protects.

This introduction shows the voice and length intended for the series: three short paragraphs that say who the person is, why we sought them out, and what the conversation turns on. The editor writes it, not the subject.

In a finished entry this paragraph places the participant in their work (the practice, the place, the years) with facts that can be checked.

The last paragraph names the question underneath. For this example: what is lost when drawing is hurried, and who bears the cost later.
