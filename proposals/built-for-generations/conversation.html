No. 001 An architect Craft Editorial example

Drawing slowly

A working title for a conversation about the hours a design spends on paper before anyone builds it, and what that patience protects.

Introduction · Example text

This introduction shows the voice and length intended for the series: three short paragraphs that tell the reader who the person is, why we sought them out, and what the conversation turns on. It is written by the editor, not the subject.

In a finished entry this paragraph would place the participant in their work — the practice, the place, the years — with facts that can be checked rather than adjectives that cannot.

The last paragraph names the question underneath the conversation. For this example: what is lost when drawing is hurried, and who bears the cost later.
