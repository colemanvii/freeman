01PositionWho Freeman is

Freeman General Contractors is a general contractor based in Cartersville, Georgia, building hospitality, commercial, retail, healthcare and high-end residential projects.

Relationships are the bedrock of the work. Freeman brings the same respect, integrity and commitment from personal life to every interaction — with clients, subcontractors, suppliers, architects and engineers. Those connections are the foundation for every project.

    05MethodWhat distinguishes the approach

    Constructing
    Hospitality

    Constructing Hospitality means caring for people throughout the build: listening to owners, respecting the design team and the trades, and considering how a place will be used.

    Thoughtful sequencing, clear communication and attention to detail are part of that service — whether the building is a restaurant, a business or a home. It is also why the record matters: progress, field photographs and decisions are kept so the work is easy to follow from one conversation to the next. Afield is the public part of that record.
