# Brand System 001

Three directions for the Freeman identity, shown through the same six applications.

- **A Refined**: the production identity held to fixed values (Monaco, Chalk/Umber, Signal as locator).
- **B Editorial**: one serif (Newsreader), monograph spreads, Signal only as the full stop of Built for Generations.
- **C Document**: sheets with title blocks, schedules and general notes (Martian Mono); the Stamp eagle as seal; field evidence in monochrome with Signal redlines.

Open `/studies/brand-system-001/`. Click any composition for the full-size viewer: ← → direction, ↑ ↓ application, F format, Z fit / 1:1, Esc. Deep links: `#B-02-D` (direction, application number, D/M/F/R/S).

## Sources

- Content is read from `/records.js` and copied from `/app.js` (Company, people, services, in-progress list, contact). Field-note observations are those published in `field-notes.html`.
- The signature is drawn from the production bitmap at runtime (`/pixel-mark.js`); the eagle is the production contact-eagle path (`marks.js`). Both appear at whole-pixel scales only.
- Field images in `field/` are copies of `field-notes-assets/`. Left out on purpose: the AI-generated radius-window sequence and the historical steelwork photograph (neither is Freeman evidence), and `assets/project-7.jpg` (not assigned to a project).
- Unrecorded projects and dates are stated as unrecorded. The jobsite sign uses Little Rey, an in-progress project from the production list; no architect or dates are claimed.

## Recommendation

The closing plate recommends developing C (Document), held to A's restraint, and beginning dated captures on Little Rey so the first field sheets carry real evidence.
