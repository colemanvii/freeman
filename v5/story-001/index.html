Rules of this study

  1. Only real material. No invented photographs, credits, client statements, dates, quantities or construction facts.
  2. Photographs are the originals from Freeman’s published portfolio, unaltered. Captions and keynotes describe only what is visible.
  3. No photograph has been matched to Freeman’s scope, so no visible element is attributed to Freeman’s work.
  4. Design credit belongs to Chuck Marie Studio. Freeman is credited for construction only.
  5. Square footage (2,000+) and completion year (2024) appear only in legacy template credits. The live site omits them; so do these studies.
  6. Missing evidence is shown as missing, never filled in.
